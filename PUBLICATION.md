# Publication scope

The public project is https://github.com/Kohze/numerical-range-certificates. The [version 0.1.0 alpha release](https://github.com/Kohze/numerical-range-certificates/releases/tag/v0.1.0) accompanies the numerical technical note in `paper/` and includes source, installation files and supporting data.

The contribution is a finite exact-rational certificate workflow, explicit theorem provenance and reproducible error diagnostics. The geometry and underlying inequalities build on established work. The classical Richardson constant-two bound is unconditional and predates the recent mathematics release. General-polynomial sharp constant-two certificates remain conditional in this project's assurance convention.

On the coupled fixture, numerical-range construction certifies 31 steps versus 113 from a single-step induced-norm rate and 11 from direct powers. The prototype does not demonstrate a general practical advantage over its successful competing baselines. Current theory permits matrix-valued coefficients; current software supports real-rational scalar coefficients.

Robin Gounder confirmed author critical review on 7 October 2026. The manuscript retains its dedicated generative-AI disclosure section. Computational tests and independently implemented finite checks document the software and experimental results.

The public bundle provides exact requests/certificates, retained scientific data, source and reproduction programs. A future specialist-journal submission requires a substantiated research contribution beyond the present technical note. A future JOSS route requires real sustained public development and demonstrated research use; a fresh repository or successful test suite does not establish that eligibility.
